ABOUT

Where curiosity meet orbit.

The Bhumi Drishti Project is a student-led CubeSat initiative at IISER Bhopal dedicated to designing, building, and launching advanced nanosatellites for Earth observation and space research.

From CAD design and embedded systems development to real-time ground station communications and payload data processing, we build space systems from the ground up.

We are establishing core satellite concepts and engineering frameworks, setting the stage for practical hardware workshops and subsystem integration.
